**Support back end — BANTAY (SC-18, added 2026-07-27):** three rungs — BANTAY (free, first
human reply ≤48h, bundled for life), BANTAY PLUS (₱600/mo or ₱6,000/yr, ≤12h), BANTAY 24
(₱2,499/mo, ≤1h, aimed at livelihood/commercial buyers, sold only where ≥8 systems sit within
~20 min). **HYPOTHESIS** until its pre-registered threshold clears: ≥4 of the first 10 signed
customers tick a paid tier at signing (`ops/channel-tests.md`). Non-negotiable design rule:
anything that is **our fault** — warranty, workmanship, anything unsafe — is fixed at top-tier
speed on the free rung, forever; paid rungs buy speed only on what is *not* our fault. Two
§1.6 hard stops attached: no loaner-inverter promise before a spare is physically in the
workshop (unlocks at 25 PLUS subscribers), and no published response window before a licensed
electrician/REE is retained with their PRC number printed. Year-one job is **close rate on the
₱99,500**, not revenue — 5 installs at 40% attach is ₱1,200/mo. Full write-up:
`/internal/support-moat`.

**Front-end / back-end note (§3, Ready-Fire-Aim):** the ₱99,500 install is the front end
— it holds the corridor price (no hardware discounting in a scam-wary market). Profit is
the back end, each line EVIDENCED only after its pre-registered threshold. Model A waitlist
= demand evidence only, never bookable revenue.
