MACC Field Kit — Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 August 2026 · MACC Systems & Engineering Inc., Talustusan, Naval, Biliran, Philippines

MACC Field Kit is an internal work tool for the staff of MACC Systems & Engineering Inc. It is distributed unlisted on the Chrome Web Store so our own team can install and update it; it is not a consumer product and we do not market it to the public.

We operate no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services inside this extension. There is no tracking code of any kind, and the extension makes no network request to any server other than the one described below.

What the extension stores, and where

DataWhere it goesWhy
Your outreach profile — name, job title, work email, phone, and company address Your own browser only, via chrome.storage.sync. It syncs between your own signed-in Chrome installations through Google. It is never sent to us. So the extension can type your details into a supplier's contact form instead of you retyping them.
Product and supplier details from a page you are viewing — title, price, supplier name, location, and the page URL Sent, only when you click Save, to your employer's own MACC Command Center, authenticated with your existing staff login. Nowhere else. So sourcing research is recorded, dated and attributable instead of living in screenshots and chat messages.
The address of your MACC Command Center, and any rows waiting to upload because you were offline Your own browser, via chrome.storage.sync and chrome.storage.local. Configuration, and so a capture made without a connection is not lost.

What it never does

  • It does not run while you browse. It reads a page only in the moment you click its toolbar button, using Chrome's activeTab permission. It has no access to any other tab, and none to a tab you have not clicked it on.
  • It does not record your browsing history, your clicks, your keystrokes, or anything you do on pages where you have not used it.
  • It never submits a form for you. It fills fields; you read them and send it yourself.
  • It never reads or fills passwords, captchas, checkboxes or radio buttons.
  • It does not sell or transfer your data to anyone. There is no third party to transfer it to.
  • It does not use your data for creditworthiness or lending purposes, or for any purpose unrelated to the single purpose above.

Who can see the data

Your outreach profile is visible only to you, in your own browser. Product captures are visible to MACC Systems & Engineering Inc. staff with access to the Command Center, and each capture records which staff member saved it — the same way any other action in that system is attributed.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored in your browser, including your outreach profile and any queued rows. Product captures already saved to the Command Center belong to the company's records; ask a MACC administrator to delete a row, or delete it yourself from the captures page.
