Field Kit browser extension

One toolbar button that does two jobs: saves a supplier listing into Product captures, and fills a manufacturer's contact form with your details and a drafted message. Install it once, then it is two clicks instead of twenty minutes of retyping.

0 Check you can run it

Desktop Chrome, Edge, Brave or any Chromium browser. Extensions do not exist on Chrome for Android or iOS, and this will not run in Safari or Firefox.

Working from a phone today? Skip the extension. Capture what you need by hand, or use the Copy row button on a laptop later — nothing here is the only way to get a row into Captures.

1 Get the extension

Chrome Web Store · in review

Submitted 4 August 2026. Once Google approves it, the install link goes here and in the group channel — one click, and Chrome keeps it updated for you afterwards. Review usually takes a few days.

Until then, load it from the repo:

  1. Get the solar city repo onto your machine (git pull if you already have it).
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode, top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the folder tools/product-scraper-extension.
  4. Pin MACC Field Kit to the toolbar — puzzle-piece icon, then the pin.

Loaded this way, Chrome never updates it. When the group channel says a new build landed: git pull, then Reload on the Field Kit card at chrome://extensions. Once you install from the Store instead, remove the unpacked copy — two copies means two icons and two separate offline queues.

2 Connect it to the Command Center

  1. Right-click the extension icon → Options.
  2. Paste the address you use for the Command Center — the same one you are reading this page on — and click Save settings.
  3. Chrome asks whether the extension may talk to that site. Click Allow. Saving captures fails without it.
  4. Make sure you are signed in here with your own login, not the shared team password. Captures are attributed, and "Shared team login" tells nobody who found the supplier.
  5. Click Test connection. You want "Connected ✓ — signed in, captures API reachable."

There is no extra password or API key anywhere in the extension. It talks to the Command Center as you, using the session you are already signed in with — which is also why every capture lands with your name on it.

3 Fill in your outreach profile

Still in Options, under Outreach profile. The company details — name, address, website, city, province — are already filled in and the same for everyone. Add the ones that are yours: first and last name, job title, work email, phone.

Use my Command Center name & email copies those two across from your account so you do not retype them. Then Save profile.

This lives in your own browser and is never sent to the Command Center.

4 Capture tab — saving a supplier listing

On an Alibaba listing, a Facebook Marketplace item, a Shopee or Lazada page, any shop: click the icon, check what it read, add a line in Notes saying why it matters, and hit Save to Command Center. The row appears on Product captures with today's date and the source URL.

  • Facebook group posts: select the post text first, then click the icon. Facebook's markup cannot be read reliably, so your selection is the capture.
  • Check the price kept its full range and unit — "US $28.50 – 32.00 / piece", not just "28.50".
  • No signal, or your session expired? The row is queued on your machine and the icon shows a count. Sign in, then Retry queued.

A capture is evidence, not a quote. It records what a listing said on a date. Nothing from Captures goes into a customer quote until a supplier confirms it in writing.

5 Outreach tab — filling a brand's contact form

On a manufacturer's contact or "become a distributor" page — Sungrow, Deye, Solis, anyone — click the icon, switch to Outreach, pick the message, and click Fill this form. Filled fields get an amber outline, and a list under the buttons shows exactly what it wrote and what it left for you.

MessageUse it for
Biliran co-marketing eventWe host an informational event in Naval; they supply literature, a banner or demo unit, and ideally a speaker — and become our preferred brand for what we install here.
Become a distributorDistribution or dealer terms for the Philippines: tiers, MOQ, trade pricing, warranty service path.
Installer / dealer accountOpen a buying account, or get pointed at their authorised Philippine distributor.
Technical docs & complianceDatasheets, IEC 62109 and anti-islanding certificates — what a net-metering submission needs.

It never submits anything. It fills the form; you read it, tick any consent box yourself, and press send. Undo puts every field back. It will not touch passwords, captchas, checkboxes or radio buttons, and it leaves alone any field that already has a value.

Edit the draft, but keep it honest. Every message says plainly that we are newly incorporated and pre-revenue, and promises no volumes. That is deliberate: a brand that finds out later stops replying, and an inflated claim costs us the relationship. Never add install counts, certifications or distributor status we do not have.

6 If something breaks

It saysDo this
Signed out — row queuedThe row is safe. Sign in here, reopen the popup, Retry queued.
App unreachable — row queuedNo signal or the app is down. Queued rows also retry on browser restart.
Site permission was declinedOptions → Save settings again → Allow on Chrome's prompt.
This build isn't allowed to talk to …The Command Center moved to a domain the extension does not know. Post in the group channel — it needs a one-line change and a reload.
Nothing auto-fills on a Facebook postSelect the post text first, then click the icon.
Nothing auto-fills on a contact formThe site names its fields in a way the extension cannot read. Type them, or use Copy to take the draft and email them instead.
That section is not available on your accountCaptures is hidden from your login. Ask a founder to tick it under Team & access → Sections. Outreach still works.

Anything else: post it in the group channel, not a DM — same rule as everything else here.
