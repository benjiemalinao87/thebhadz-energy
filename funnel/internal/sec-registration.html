MAPTwo chains — don't confuse them

Everything below belongs to one of two chains. Chain 1 registers the company and is done once (then renewed annually). Chain 2 permits each installation and is repeated per job, per LGU, per homeowner. A complete Chain 1 does not let you energize a roof; a complete Chain 2 without Chain 1 is not a company. The §7 deposit blocker sits across both.

How many founders hold equity? 1 → One Person Corporation (OPC) eligible. 2+ → regular domestic stock corporation (min. 2 incorporators, no statutory minimum capital for most activities).

BFP — the Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC)

The FSIC is a Bureau of Fire Protection clearance under RA 9514 (Fire Code of the Philippines). It covers our own premises — the office and, later, the workshop — and the Mayor's permit cannot be issued or renewed without it. Validity is one year, so it renews on the same annual cycle as the business permit. It is not part of the per-install permit chain for an ordinary residential rooftop job; that chain is the LGU electrical permit and CFEI in §6.

PCAB, DOE, electrician, net metering

PCAB — the one that actually gates contracting

PCAB (under CIAP/DTI) licenses the business to take construction and electrical contracting work; PRC licenses the people who sign the plans. A new contractor with no track record enters at the bottom category and needs a Sustaining Technical Employee — we have one in-house: Jundhel Cabradilla, Registered Electrical Engineer, PRC No. 0050698, 12 years as project electrical engineer / site supervisor. Two questions still to put to PCAB directly rather than assume: (a) does the classification covering solar PV / electrical installation accept an REE as STE, or require a PEE; (b) at what system capacity does plan sign-off hand off from an REE to a PEE (residential rooftop is expected to sit inside REE scope — verify). Answer those and §7's licensed-sign-off blocker on accepting a deposit is cleared. EST

DOE — corrected: strongly wanted, not legally required EST

An earlier revision of this page treated DOE accreditation as a blocker on quoting. Public 2026 sources say otherwise: DOE listing is not legally mandated for private residential installations. Where it does bind is government procurement — public agencies buying solar under RA 9184 are required to use DOE-listed installers — and as a trust signal a scam-wary homeowner (and some DUs and financiers) will check. The DOE registry listed roughly 92 solar PV installers as of Jan 2026, so it is a real, attainable list, and RESC accreditation under the Renewable Energy Act is the related company-level registration. Treat it as: apply, don't wait for it.

Honesty rule (§1.6) Do not print, imply, or let a Messenger reply suggest "DOE-accredited" until the certificate exists and its number can be quoted. Same for PCAB. The legally required set for a private residential rooftop job is PCAB + a PRC-licensed signing professional + the LGU electrical permit and CFEI + (grid-tied only) the DU net-metering approval — say that, and nothing more.
