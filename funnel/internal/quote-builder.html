Their BILECO bill

Ask to see last month's bill rather than asking what they usually pay — the exact figure sizes the system properly and the conversation stays past-specific. Rate and yield are both marked EST in SC-08; replace the yield with metered output once the first install is running.

System

Coverage starts at whatever the chosen package is set to in Packages & pricing. On-grid is deliberately under 100% because export is credited below the retail rate — size for daytime self-consumption first. Use the panel override once the roof survey gives you the real usable area.

Money

Market price helper
Suggested selling price₱—

Competitor price = what an established installer charges per kW, all-in (Lacosta ≈₱70,000/kW, seen 2026-07 — reverify before sending). For systems bigger than our fixed packages, this is how the selling price is set: from what the market already charges, never from our own costs.

Profit floor & take-home settings

Take-home = profit − referral fee (₱/kW, per meeting #7) − warranty fund − tax set-aside. These four start from Packages & pricing and can be overridden for one quote here. Leave tax at 0 while the VAT box above is ticked; if MACC registers non-VAT, untick VAT and set ~3% here — confirm with the accountant. The minimum profit is a placeholder until the founders agree a house number in ops/status.md.

Materials pass through at supplier cost; our profit sits in the fee line. The contract price defaults to the fixed package prices — and for bigger custom systems it comes from the market price helper above, never from the sheet total. Pricing from our cost + a markup is banned (§3).

Packages

What the founder picks in System. The two switches are not cosmetic: grid-tied decides whether BILECO net-metering appears in the checklist and the milestones, and needs battery decides whether storage and a hybrid inverter are offered — and whether quoting it without a battery is flagged as selling backup that does not exist. A system with no battery always carries the brownout disclosure either way (§1.6). Founder OS §3 holds the offer to three packages in the ₱80–100k window; a fourth needs its own pre-registered threshold before a customer sees it.

Price ladder

A quote takes the first tier it fits inside — both ceilings, size and storage. Anything bigger falls through to CUSTOM, where the price is set from the market rather than from our costs (§3). Tiers are sorted smallest-first on save.

Sizing assumptions

These decide what the customer is told they will save. Self-consumption is the share of what the panels make that actually comes off the bill — without storage the rest exports below retail, so counting it in full would overstate the saving (§1.6).

Money

The fee is what the customer is charged for labour; our delivery cost is what that labour actually costs us. The gap between them is the profit, so they are two separate numbers on purpose.
